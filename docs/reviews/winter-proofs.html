Winter — proposed drawings (Nov 13, 2026)

For docs/reviews/winter-design.md and winter-animation.md. Every piece is drawn in the board's own palette (no new colour: snow is the board's white, its shadows are pale turquoise, ice is the sea's own; the warm notes are November's rust and amber and October's lit yellow). Section 1 is at TRUE size with the boat; the close-ups say their scale; the four composites at the end are the real Deckhand.html, photographed at winter dates with its own clock override, with the winter drawings laid over it. winter-proofs-8m.png is this sheet at one third: the 8 m check.

1 · At true size, with the boat (1080p board pixels)

On the water, everything is the boat's size or smaller (the boat's box is 38 px, its hull 30): the seal on its floe 52 px (near lane, Wilson's spot), the pancake floes 18–44 px, the narwhal 60 px in the far lane behind it. On the sand, things are nearer the room and stand at the scarecrow's scale: the snowman 96 × 134 px at the right edge. Flakes fall at the leaves' scale (44–62 px; Croix found 34–46 px leaves "small"), with 8–12 px flecks between.
