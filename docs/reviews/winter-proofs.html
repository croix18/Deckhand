Winter 2.0: proposed drawings (Nov 14, 2026)

For docs/reviews/winter-design.md and winter-animation.md. This is the redesign after the audit (winter-audit.md) and Croix's picks: a manatee on a Florida sea, string lights across the top of the page, snow as weather, a snowman of beach finds, frost "decently sized at full peak", and no squall. Winter's one new color is ice blue #4C7BD9, with its tints #C9D9F7 (shadow on snow) and #EEF3FE (frosted glass). Every snow shape carries a navy edge. Section 1 is at TRUE size beside the live November board. The composites are the real Deckhand.html 7.63.0, photographed at winter dates with its own clock override, with the winter drawings laid over it. winter-proofs-8m.png is this sheet at one third (the 8 m check).
