    Bells

    The week rotation picks this (below).
    Nudge shifts only the bell math, never the clock. The warning turns the countdown coral and pulses the bar; 0 turns it off.
    Week rotation
    Weeks alternate between the first two week types from the anchor Monday, counting school weeks: a week with no school days (Thanksgiving, winter break, spring break) doesn’t flip the colour. A week with a single holiday still counts. Tapping a chip on the home screen still overrides for the day; Wednesdays switch times on their own.
    Clock readout
    What the clock shows under the time. “Next class after hours” counts down to the next school day once the last bell has rung.
    Settle-in at the bell
    The clock becomes the count when a class begins (Lunch excepted), then hands off to a Slides card if the scene has one. While it runs, Space restarts it and R stands it down. A scene needs a clock to run it.
    Wrap-up at the bell
    The clock takes the stage with the time to the bell, the exit ticket, the checklist (tap a line to check it off) and the rule; at the bell the face comes back. R stands it down for that period.
    Bathroom window

    Timer

    One to six values, separated by commas. These are the chips on every timer and the ⏱ menu in stage mode.
    How the time left is drawn behind the digits.
    Needs a Music card on the board. The music stops when the timer ends or pauses.

    Rosters

    One box per period — first names separated by commas or new lines. The name picker and group maker follow the bell schedule; leave a box empty to skip that period. To move a student, cut the name from one box and paste it into another.
    Pulls first names from the Seating Chart tool by period number into the boxes below. Apply to keep them.
    Privacy: an exported copy carries student names inside it — keep exports in your own Drive and never upload one to the public website (GitHub).

    Schedules
