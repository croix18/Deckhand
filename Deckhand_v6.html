General
This device asks for reduced motion — on Auto, the water holds still.
Clock & Bells
With rotation on, the home screen pre-picks the week from the anchor Monday (weeks alternate between the first two week types); tapping a chip still overrides for the day. Wednesdays switch times automatically. Nudge shifts only the bell math. The end-of-class warning turns the countdown coral and pulses the bar.
Timer
Class Rosters
One box per period — student first names separated by commas or new lines. The name picker and group maker follow the bell schedule automatically; leave a box empty to skip that period. Download a configured copy to keep rosters in the file. Privacy: a copy with rosters carries student names inside it — keep those copies in your own Drive, and never upload one to the public website (GitHub).
Week Schedules
Up to four week types, each with regular times and (optionally) Wednesday times. One block per line: label start-end (e.g. 3rd 11:46-12:39). Times 6:00–11:59 are AM, 12:00–5:59 are PM. Empty regular box = week type removed; empty Wednesday box = Wednesdays use the regular times.
"Download configured copy" saves a new Deckhand file with these settings baked in — replace your old file with it and the settings are permanent. Nothing is stored in the browser.
